# Privacy — Fresh Ink

Fresh Ink's data practices: booking details only, no accounts, no data sale.

- **What we collect:** the booking details you provide when holding a slot (name, email, phone, and your tattoo idea). That's it.
- **No accounts:** there is nothing to sign up for, so no profiles or passwords are stored.
- **No data sale:** booking details are never sold or shared with advertisers.
- **Rate limiting:** minimal request counters keep the service stable; they expire automatically.

Questions about privacy: studio@freshink.art.
